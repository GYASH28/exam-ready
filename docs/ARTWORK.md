# Theme artwork

All six WebP scene files in `android/app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/` were generated for this personal build on 4 October 2026. They are bundled assets and require no network access.

- `theme_naruto.webp`: Naruto at a Hidden Leaf sunset with a Rasengan.
- `theme_bleach.webp`: Ichigo, moonlit rooftops and blade energy.
- `theme_blackclover.webp`: Asta, grimoire and anti-magic imagery.
- `theme_demonslayer.webp`: Tanjiro, water breathing and wisteria.
- `theme_onepiece.webp`: Luffy, sunset ocean and pirate sails.
- `theme_dragonball.webp`: an original cosmic mountain training environment, ringed planet and golden-blue energy. The character-scene generation did not succeed; this asset intentionally contains no franchise character.

Images were exported to 1200×800 WebP at quality 88 for the APK. Their decoded bitmap cache is bounded to six entries. Theme cards use a center crop, rounded clipping and a dark text scrim; ambient backdrops use low-opacity artwork with signature motifs.

Franchise character rights remain with their respective owners. These are generated fan-art scenes, not official licensed franchise assets.
